# TeamSync AI: Live Demonstration & Evaluation Panel Script
## Student Project Team Formation & Contribution Intelligence Platform
**Submission Deadline:** 8 November 2026  
**Audience:** College Evaluation Committee, Faculty Advisors, and Project Reviewers

---

### Step 1: Platform Overview & Problem Statement (Time: 1.5 mins)
1. Open the landing page at `http://localhost:3000`.
2. Highlight the problem:
   - College teams frequently fail due to convenience-based grouping, leading to missing skill sets, scheduling collisions, unequal workload, and bitter disputes.
3. Show the tagline: **"Build Better Teams. Create Better Projects."**
4. Walk through the **Formula Breakdown** on the landing page:
   - $M = 0.40S + 0.25A + 0.15I + 0.10R + 0.10W$
   - Emphasize that all scores are 100% explainable and mathematically normalized, not opaque black-box guesses.

---

### Step 2: Browse Projects & Competencies (Time: 2 mins)
1. Navigate to **Projects** (`/projects`).
2. Show the domain filtering (Web Development, AI/ML, FinTech, IoT).
3. Select **"TeamSync AI: Intelligent Student Collaboration Platform"** (`/projects/proj-teamsync`).
4. Review the defined requirements:
   - Required skills (Next.js Lvl 4+, PostgreSQL Lvl 3+, Figma Lvl 3+)
   - Weekly sync target (4 hours/week)
   - Milestones checklist

---

### Step 3: Algorithmic Recommendations & What-If Simulator (Time: 3 mins)
*This is the core differentiator of the project.*
1. Click **"Find Matched Teams"** or navigate to `/projects/proj-teamsync/matches`.
2. View the candidate cohorts generated by the greedy matching engine:
   - **Optimal Synergy Team** (Score: 94%)
   - **Technical Depth Team** (Highest Skill Coverage)
   - **High Availability Cohort** (Max Overlapping Hours)
3. Expand the **What-If Team Simulator** tab:
   - Show the dynamic sliders for Skills, Availability, Interest, Role, and Workload.
   - Adjust the **Availability slider to 60%** and observe the real-time score adjustment.
   - Remove a member from the simulated team and click **Add** on an AI candidate from the candidate pool.
   - Point out the instant update of the **Skill Coverage**, **Overlap Hours**, and **Workload Feasibility meters**.

---

### Step 4: Kanban Workspace & Independent Review Guardrail (Time: 2.5 mins)
1. Navigate to **Team Workspace** (`/teams/team-alpha/tasks`).
2. Show the 4-column Kanban board: *To Do*, *In Progress*, *In Review*, *Completed*.
3. Click **"Create Task"** to demonstrate task assignment, estimated hours, and acceptance criteria.
4. On an in-progress task, click **"Submit Deliverable"**:
   - Provide PR link: `https://github.com/teamsync-ai/core/pull/40`
   - Show how the task transitions to **In Review**.
5. Demonstrate the **Self-Approval Prevention Guardrail**:
   - Click **"Review Deliverable"** while logged in as the author; attempt to approve it.
   - Show the server-side rejection: *"Self-approval forbidden. Deliverables require an independent peer or reviewer evaluation."*
   - Log in or switch to an independent peer reviewer, approve the deliverable with feedback, and show the task move to **Completed**.

---

### Step 5: Contribution Intelligence & Fairness Disputes (Time: 2 mins)
1. Navigate to **Analytics** (`/teams/team-alpha/analytics`).
2. Show the live **Workload Distribution Bar Chart** (assigned hours vs completed hours) powered by Recharts.
3. Point out the **Task Execution Breakdown Pie Chart** and the **Verified Contribution Points Index**.
4. Navigate to **Fairness & Disputes** (`/teams/team-alpha/feedback`).
5. Demonstrate the **Formal Dispute Workflow**:
   - Show an open dispute: *"Missing recorded credit for schema review session"*.
   - Click **"Mediate & Record Resolution"**, add faculty/lead mediation notes, and click **Resolve**.
   - Show that an immutable audit log is generated with timestamped credit.

---

### Step 6: Architecture, Testing & Code Quality (Time: 1.5 mins)
1. Open the terminal and run:
   ```bash
   npm test
   ```
2. Display the **13 passing automated test suites**:
   - 7 Matching Engine unit tests (weights validation, missing critical skills, schedule penalty, interest alignment, role coverage, workload overload warnings, full team greedy selection).
   - 6 Integration tests (password hashing, JWT verification, user creation, team limits, self-approval prevention, dispute resolution).
3. Conclude by showing the production build verification and maintainable directory structure.
